You play as the strongest mage in history, but the age of magic is over, so you have to get a job running security for small businesses against their inexplicably angry customers. Your current gig is for a cat café that seems to attract every single angry person in the town, but you have the chance to make more money by using your signature spell: turning people into cats. Your goal is to make enough money to move close enough to work at the glue factory, so you turn the angry customers into cats so your employer will pay you more for the extra business you bring. When you get enough money and try to leave, your employer tries to stop you, so you turn them into a cat too and investigate. Turns out, it was the obelisk in the basement that was attracting the angry customers, and the café owner worships it. You destroy the obelisk and drive off to your new job at the glue factory.
